## About me

<a href="https://github.com/prabhuparth442"><img align="right" width="190" src="https://github.com/prabhuparth442.png?size=380" alt="avatar" /></a>

I build things to understand how they work. My projects cover drone vision, GPS-denied drone swarms, agentic AI systems and measurement hardware, and I take part in robotics and Inter-IIT style competitions.

**Working on right now**

- Thermal UAV image to satellite geo-localisation for an Inter-IIT problem
- An onboard-only drone swarm for Robofest
- An auto-balancing bridge impedance meter with multi-method calibration
- A browser-based AI task worker that verifies its own actions

## Projects
